APPROACH 1 • NOT RECOMMENDED AS DEFAULT

Direct Parallelism — Airflow → 200 Spark Jobs → Kubernetes

Ab Initio modernization baseline: a near lift-and-shift execution model where eligible DAG tasks directly launch containerized PySpark/Spark workloads.

Advantages

  • Lowest implementation complexity.
  • Maximum theoretical parallelism and shortest completion time when infrastructure is unconstrained.
  • Airflow directly maps DAG-ready tasks to Kubernetes execution.

Concerns

  • Sizes infrastructure for peak rather than SLA.
  • Large burst can create CPU/memory, image-pull, network and downstream contention.
  • Over-requested Spark containers multiply waste across hundreds of jobs.
  • Today's “200” becomes a hard capacity assumption as workload grows.

Why We Would Not Choose It by Default

  • Workload count ≠ required concurrency.
  • Does not exploit 8–10 years of legacy runtime/dependency evidence to find actual concurrency needs.
  • Use only when SLA explicitly requires near-full parallelism and load testing proves downstream systems can sustain it.
Architecture message: Kubernetes may be capable of running 200 jobs concurrently; the architectural question is whether the SLA requires us to provision and pay for that peak.
